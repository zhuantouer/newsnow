## 📌 六、前沿论文精选（arXiv / alphaXiv 本周热门）

### ⭐ 1. ELF: Embedded Language Flows
- **作者**: Keya Hu, Linlu Qiu, Yiyang Lu et al.
- **热度**: 109📌 / 1882 views / GitHub 132⭐
- **核心**: 基于 Flow Matching 的连续扩散语言模型，训练仅需基线 **1/10 token 数量** 即达到同等性能
- **意义**: 扩散式语言模型效率突破，可能重塑 LLM 预训练范式。**与 zishuai 关注的 dLLM/扩散生成模型方向高度相关**

### ⭐ 2. Qwen-Image-2.0 Technical Report
- **作者**: Bing Zhao, Chenfei Wu, Deqing Li et al. (Alibaba)
- **热度**: 54📌 / 893 views
- **核心**: 统一图像生成与编辑，2K+ 分辨率写实，LMArena ELO 1168，SOTA VAE 重建
- **意义**: 多模态统一架构的工业级验证，生成+理解一体化持续进化

### ⭐ 3. Evolving-RL: End-to-End Self-Evolving Agents
- **作者**: Zhiyuan Fan, Wenwei Jin, Feng Zhang (小红书 / 北大)
- **热度**: 30📌 / 342 views
- **核心**: LLM Agent 从历史经验中提取和复用 skill 的端到端 RL 框架
- **意义**: Agent 自进化能力的实用化路径，**与推荐系统中 Agent 辅助决策场景相关**

### ⭐ 4. Solve the Loop: Attractor Models for Language and Reasoning
- **作者**: Jacob Fein-Ashley, Paria Rashidinejad
- **热度**: 29📌 / 466 views
- **核心**: 将语言推理建模为不动点问题，通过迭代计算收敛到解
- **意义**: 为 reasoning 提供新的数学框架，突破传统自回归范式

### ⭐ 5. SlimQwen: Pruning and Distillation in Large MoE Pre-training
- **作者**: Shengkun Tang, Zekun Wang, Bo Zheng (Alibaba / MBZUAI / KAUST)
- **热度**: 25📌 / 349 views
- **核心**: MoE 模型 4x 尺寸压缩 / 72% 峰值显存削减 / 48% 吞吐提升
- **意义**: MoE 大模型高效部署的关键技术，**直接适用于推荐系统中的大规模 MoE 预估模型压缩**

### ⭐ 6. The Truth Lies in the Middle (of the Generated Tokens)
- **作者**: Sophie L. Wang, Phillip Isola, Brian Cheung (MIT)
- **热度**: 23📌 / 308 views
- **核心**: 对生成 token 的 hidden state 做 mean pooling 可获得高质量语义表征，跨视觉-语言/推理/蛋白质领域验证
- **意义**: 简洁有力的表征工程技巧，可直接应用于 embedding 提取

### ⭐ 7. Unmasking On-Policy Distillation: Where It Helps, Where It Hurts
- **作者**: Mohammadreza Armandpour, Fatih Ilhan, David Harrison (Apple)
- **热度**: 21📌 / 226 views
- **核心**: Token 级分析 OPD 在 LLM 中的成功/失败条件
- **意义**: 蒸馏实践的重要参考，Apple 内部大规模蒸馏经验总结

### ⭐ 8. World Action Models: The Next Frontier in Embodied AI

### 精读正文（含标注）

**Cerebras Systems, a maker of artificial intelligence chips, priced its IPO at $185 per share on Tuesday, above its expected range of $150 to $160.**

`[priced its IPO: 为其首次公开募股定价——注意 price 此处作动词用]`
`[above its expected range: 超出预期区间——金融新闻中常见的 IPO 定价表述]`

**The company sold 30 million shares, raising $5.55 billion. It's one of the largest tech IPOs in years — bigger than Snowflake's 2020 offering but smaller than Rivian's 2021 debut.**

`[offering: 发行/发售——IPO 语境中指"股票发售"]`
`[debut: 首次亮相——此处指公司首次上市交易，常用于 IPO 语境]`

**Cerebras will begin trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol CBRS on Wednesday. The fully diluted market capitalization stands at $56.4 billion.**

`[fully diluted market capitalization: 全稀释市值——计入所有期权/可转换证券后的市值，区别于 basic market cap]`
`[ticker symbol: 股票代码——交易所中标识证券的缩写字母]`

**The IPO caps a remarkable journey for the Silicon Valley startup founded in 2016. CEO Andrew Feldman's stake is now worth approximately $1.9 billion.**

`[caps: 为……画上句号/达到顶点——此处指 IPO 完成了公司上市之旅]`
`[remarkable journey: 非凡历程——记者对公司曲折上市路的概括性评价]`

**Enthusiasm has been building for the company's debut, which could be followed later this year by much bigger offerings from SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic.**

`[enthusiasm has been building: 热情一直在积聚——现在完成进行时表持续累积]`
`[could be followed by: 可能紧随其后——被动语态+情态动词表推测]`

**The company is hitting the market during a silicon renaissance. Intel, AMD and Micron have each climbed more than 80% in the past month as investor chip bets expand beyond Nvidia to the broader semiconductor universe.**

`[hitting the market: 登陆市场——非正式用法，比 entering the market 更有力度]`
`[silicon renaissance: 硅片复兴——比喻半导体行业重新繁荣]`
`[chip bets: 芯片押注——投资者对芯片股的投资/赌注]`
`[broader semiconductor universe: 更广泛的半导体版图——universe 比喻整个生态系统]`

**Cerebras had filed to go public in September 2024 but withdrew its submission in October 2025, primarily over scrutiny about heavy reliance on a single customer — G42, a Microsoft-backed company based in the United Arab Emirates.**

`[filed to go public: 提交上市申请——file 在法律/金融语境中意为"正式提交"]`
`[withdrew its submission: 撤回申请——withdraw 过去式 withdrew]`
`[scrutiny: 审查/仔细审视——指投资者和监管机构对招股书的细致检查]`
`[heavy reliance on: 严重依赖——reliance on 固定搭配]`

**In 2024, G42 accounted for 85% of Cerebras' revenue. Last year, G42's share fell to 24%, while Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in the UAE represented 62% of revenue.**

`[accounted for: 占（比例）——financial reporting 中的标准用法]`
`[represented: 占/代表——与 accounted for 同义替换，避免重复]`

**Cerebras had started shifting its focus away from selling hardware systems and more toward providing a cloud service based on its chips, putting it in competition with Google, Microsoft, Oracle and CoreWeave.**

`[shifting its focus away from … and more toward: 将重心从……转向……——常见的战略叙述结构]`
`[putting it in competition with: 使其与……形成竞争——现在分词作结果状语]`

**Cerebras scored a big win in January when it signed a deal with OpenAI worth over $20 billion for 750 megawatts of computing capacity.**

`[scored a big win: 拿下一个大单/取得重大胜利——score 作动词表"赢得"]`
`[worth over $20 billion: 价值超过 200 亿美元——后置修饰 deal]`
`[computing capacity: 算力——此处以功率(MW)衡量数据中心规模]`

**Weeks before Cerebras' IPO, Arm and SoftBank both attempted to acquire it, Bloomberg reported on Tuesday. Cerebras declined to comment.**

`[attempted to acquire: 尝试收购——attempted 暗示未成功]`
`[declined to comment: 拒绝置评——公关标准用语]`

**The 2017 discussions between OpenAI and Cerebras about a potential merger resurfaced during the Elon Musk trial. In an email, OpenAI co-founder and president Greg Brockman wrote that "exclusive access to Cerebras hardware would give OpenAI an overwhelming hardware advantage over Google."**

`[resurfaced: 重新浮出水面——指旧事在审判中被重新提起]`
`[exclusive access: 独家使用权——exclusive 强调排他性]`
`[overwhelming advantage: 压倒性优势——overwhelming 形容"无法抵挡的"]`

**As of Dec. 31, 2025, Brockman held about 78,000 shares in Cerebras, worth roughly $14.4 million at the IPO price. Altman held about 89,000 shares, worth $16.5 million.**

`[as of: 截至——时间节点标记的标准表达]`
`[worth roughly: 价值约为——roughly 表大约]`

#### 重要短语/习语
| 短语 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| price an IPO above range | 定价超出预期区间 | 表明市场需求旺盛 |
| hit the market | 登陆市场/上市 | 非正式但在财经新闻中极常用 |

#### 技术写作模式
1. **数字密度叙事**：每段至少嵌入 2-3 个具体数字（$185、30M shares、$5.55B），读者无需额外搜索即可把握全貌
2. **对比锚定**：用已知 IPO（Snowflake $3.8B / Rivian $12B）作为参照系，帮读者快速定位事件量级
3. **时间线递进**：$115-125 → $150-160 → $185，三阶定价展示市场需求的动态升温
4. **隐喻标题**："AI tsunami" 用自然灾害隐喻市场洪流，兼具紧迫感和画面感

#### 语法亮点
1. **现在完成进行时表持续趋势**：*"Enthusiasm has been building for the company's debut"* — 用 has been + V-ing 强调热度在一段时间内持续增长
2. **独立分词结构作结果状语**：*"…providing a cloud service based on its chips, putting it in competition with Google"* — putting 引出结果，主语一致
3. **介词短语后置修饰**：*"a deal with OpenAI worth over $20 billion for 750 megawatts"* — worth + for 双重后置补充说明 deal 的价值和内容
4. **As of + 时间点**：*"As of Dec. 31, 2025, Brockman held…"* — 金融报告中标定时间快照的标准用法
