## 选文：Trump warns Middle East ceasefire on "life support" — Iran rejects US peace proposal

**来源**：Straits Times / AP / CNBC 综合

## 原文精读（含标注）

The ceasefire between the US and Iran reached a particularly `precarious` [precarious: 岌岌可危的——常用于描述局势不稳定] moment on May 11 as President Donald Trump said the agreement was on "massive life support" after he rejected Tehran's latest peace offer.

Speaking to reporters in the Oval Office, Mr Trump called Iran's response to his proposal "a piece of garbage" and that he `didn't even finish reading it` [语法：didn't even finish reading — "even"加强语气，表示连读完都没有]. He dismissed Tehran's `counteroffer` [counteroffer: 还价/反提案——谈判术语，对方提出的替代方案] as "stupid" and said the state of the ceasefire is "`unbelievably` weak."

> "The ceasefire is on massive life support, where the doctor walks in and says, 'Sir, your loved one has approximately a 1 per cent chance of living.'"

[修辞：vivid analogy（生动类比）——将停火协议比作ICU病人，用医生宣布存活率1%的场景表达停火几乎不可能维持]

The president's angry reaction sent oil prices `soaring` [soaring: 猛涨/飙升——财经新闻中描述价格急速上涨的标准用词] and `dashed hopes` [dashed hopes: 破灭了希望——dash作动词意为"粉碎"] that a deal could be quickly `negotiated` [negotiated: 通过谈判达成] to reopen the Strait of Hormuz to commercial shipping.

Iran's `parliamentary speaker` [parliamentary speaker: 议会议长] Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, serving as `chief negotiator` [chief negotiator: 首席谈判代表], struck a `defiant tone` [defiant tone: 强硬/挑衅的语气——defiant意为不服从的、挑战的]:

> "Our armed forces are ready to respond and to `teach a lesson` [teach a lesson: 给...一个教训] for any aggression. A bad strategy and bad decisions always lead to bad results – the world already understands this."

Iran had demanded an end to the US naval `blockade` [blockade: 封锁——军事/贸易术语，指以武力阻断航路或港口] of Iranian ports, the `cessation` [cessation: 停止/终止——正式用语，比stop更书面] of regional warfare — implying a halt to Israel's strikes targeting Hezbollah in Lebanon — and the release of `frozen assets` [frozen assets: 被冻结的资产——指被外国政府扣押禁止动用的资金] belonging to the Iranian people.

Iran's state media described Trump's plan as `tantamount to` [tantamount to: 等同于/无异于——正式书面用语] surrender and demanded the US pay `war reparations` [war reparations: 战争赔偿——国际法术语].

According to the Wall Street Journal, Iran's `counterproposal` included the possibility of `diluting` [diluting: 稀释——此处指降低铀浓缩度] some `highly enriched uranium` [highly enriched uranium (HEU): 高浓缩铀——核武相关术语，浓缩度>20%], with the rest transferred to a third country.

Oil prices settled sharply higher. `Brent crude` [Brent crude: 布伦特原油——北海基准油价，全球定价参考] gained $2.92, or 2.9%, to $104.21 per `barrel` [barrel: 桶——石油计量单位，1桶=159升], while US `West Texas Intermediate` [West Texas Intermediate (WTI): 西德克萨斯中质原油——美国基准油价] crude gained $2.65, or 2.8%, to close at $98.07. During the session, Brent briefly climbed to nearly $106 while WTI moved back above the $100 level.

The `rebound` [rebound: 反弹——价格从低位回升] followed Trump's comments and renewed threats from Iran. Ebrahim Rezaei, spokesman for the Iranian Parliament's `national security commission` [national security commission: 国家安全委员会], warned on social media:

> "Our restraint is over as of today. Any attack on our `vessels` [vessels: 船只/舰艇——正式用语] will trigger a strong and `decisive` [decisive: 果断的/决定性的] Iranian response against American ships and bases."

Amin Nasser, CEO and president of Saudi oil giant `Aramco` [Aramco: 沙特阿美——全球最大石油公司], told investors: "The energy `supply shock` [supply shock: 供给冲击——经济学术语，指供给突然减少引发的市场动荡] that began in the first quarter is the largest the world has ever experienced."

The `blockade` has created a shortage of `fertilizer` [fertilizer: 化肥——农业大宗商品] — much of which comes from Gulf ports — threatening food supplies for `tens of millions` of people. Jorge Moreira da Silva, `executive director` of the United Nations Office for Project Services, warned there were just a few weeks left to `avert` [avert: 避免/防止——正式用语，常与crisis/disaster搭配] a potentially "massive `humanitarian crisis`" [humanitarian crisis: 人道主义危机]:

> "We may `witness` a crisis that will force 45 million more people into hunger and `starvation` [starvation: 饥饿/饿死]."

Trump told Fox News he was considering `reviving` [reviving: 重启/恢复——常用于描述政策或计划的重新启动] a short-lived US operation to guide oil `tankers` [tankers: 油轮] and commercial ships through Hormuz, but had not yet made a final decision. Saudi sources previously told AFP that Saudi Arabia had `prohibited` [prohibited: 禁止——语气比banned更正式] the US from using its `airspace` [airspace: 领空] and bases for such an operation, fearing "it would just `escalate` [escalate: 升级/激化——地缘冲突高频词] the situation and would not work."

Meanwhile, Trump also proposed `suspending` [suspending: 暂停——此处为经济政策语境] the federal gas tax — currently 18.4 cents per gallon, unchanged since 1993 — to help Americans shoulder `surging` [surging: 激增的] fuel prices caused by the Iran war. The average price of gasoline in the US has climbed to approximately $4.52 per gallon, approaching the all-time recorded average of $5.02 set in June 2022.

The US issued sanctions against 12 individuals and `entities` [entities: 实体——法律/金融术语，指公司或组织] facilitating Iranian oil sales to China, including three Iran-based individuals and nine companies based in Hong Kong and the United Arab Emirates.

| WTI (West Texas Intermediate) | 西德克萨斯中质原油（美国基准） | "WTI crude gained $2.65 to close at $98.07" |

### 重要短语/习语

| 短语 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| on life support | 靠生命维持系统存活（比喻：濒临崩溃） | 形容停火协议岌岌可危 |
| dash hopes | 粉碎希望 | "dashed hopes that a deal could be quickly negotiated" |
| teach a lesson | 给...一个教训 | 伊朗军方对美方的威胁用语 |
| tantamount to | 等同于/无异于 | "described Trump's plan as tantamount to surrender" |
| soaring / surging | 飙升/激增 | 财经报道中描述价格暴涨的标准搭配 |
| avert a crisis | 避免一场危机 | "just a few weeks left to avert a humanitarian crisis" |
| strike a defiant tone | 采取强硬姿态 | "Ghalibaf struck a defiant tone" |

### 修辞与写作技巧

1. **生动类比（Vivid Analogy）**：Trump将停火比作"ICU里存活率仅1%的病人"，用具象画面传达抽象外交状态，这是政治人物制造新闻标题的经典手法。

2. **渐进升级（Escalatory Framing）**："reached a particularly precarious moment" → "on massive life support" → "1% chance of living"——三层递进将紧张感推至高潮。

3. **对比构建（Contrastive Structure）**：文章不断在美方立场和伊朗立场间切换（Trump's demands vs. Iran's demands），用平行结构呈现谈判僵局。

4. **数据锚定（Data Anchoring）**：油价变动精确到小数点（"$2.92, or 2.9%"），为感性叙事提供理性支撑，这是财经新闻的标配写法。

### 语法亮点

1. **独立主格结构（Absolute Construction）**：
   > "Speaking to reporters in the Oval Office, Mr Trump called Iran's response..."
   - 现在分词短语作状语，交代背景动作的同时推进主要叙事。

2. **同位语从句（Appositive Clause）**：
   > "Amin Nasser, CEO and president of Saudi oil giant Aramco, told investors..."
   - 用逗号间隔的同位语补充身份信息，是英文新闻高频结构。

3. **虚拟/推测语气（Subjunctive Hint）**：
   > "fearing it would just escalate the situation and would not work"
   - "would"表示对未来的推测和担忧，非事实陈述。

4. **被动语态强调结果**：
   > "Oil prices were sent soaring" / "hopes were dashed"
   - 被动结构将焦点放在"结果"而非"行为者"，这是新闻简洁性的需要。
