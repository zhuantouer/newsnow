# 📖 英文精读：Fortune — "The war with Iran is 'over' but the jet fuel crisis is about to begin"

> **选文理由**：这篇文章横跨地缘政治与能源经济，是本日最具"多源交叉验证"价值的报道——将"战争结束"的乐观叙事与"供应链尚未修复"的残酷现实并置，逻辑严密，数据翔实，语言地道。适合金融从业者精读。

**作者：Jim Edwards | Fortune | May 6, 2026**

## 原文精读（含生词/短语/语法标注）

The war with Iran is 'over' but the jet fuel crisis is about to begin

The S&P 500 was up 0.81% at a record of 7,259 on Tuesday as investors `digest` [digest: 消化（信息）——此处指市场消化好消息] news that Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced "Operation Epic Fury is `concluded`" [concluded: 已结束——正式用语，比ended更庄重] and that President Trump said Project Freedom (the ship escort operations in the Strait of Hormuz) would be "`paused` for a short period" [paused: 暂停——暗示并非永久结束，留有余地].

Iranian media celebrated what they called "America's defeat." But a number of problems remain.

The `blockade` [blockade: 封锁——海上军事封锁，指一方阻止另一方船只通行] of the Strait of Hormuz remains in place by both the U.S. and Iran. Approximately 22,500 crew members on 1,550 ships remain `stuck in the Gulf` [stuck in the Gulf: 被困在海湾——stuck表滞留/动弹不得]. Military experts express uncertainty about what happens next. The White House has declared the war "over" multiple times before — only to `resume fighting` [resume fighting: 恢复战斗——resume指中断后重新开始].

Policy rhetoric has taken "`dozens of twists and turns`" [twists and turns: 曲折反复——比喻政策反复无常] since the conflict began in February 2026.

### The real crisis: jet fuel

But perhaps the biggest immediate problem for consumers is not whether `hostilities` [hostilities: 敌对行动/交战——正式军事用语] have ended, but whether the world's fuel supply — particularly jet fuel — has been `irreversibly damaged` [irreversibly damaged: 不可逆转地受损——irreversibly强调无法恢复].

Goldman Sachs analyst Yulia Zhestkova Grigsby wrote in a note to clients: "We see large risks of jet fuel shortages in Europe already this summer." She warned that Europe's jet fuel `inventories` [inventories: 库存——此处指战略储备] will fall below the International Energy Agency's critical 23-day `shortage threshold` [shortage threshold: 短缺阈值——低于此水平即进入供应危机状态] in June.

The `Amsterdam-Rotterdam-Antwerp` [Amsterdam-Rotterdam-Antwerp (ARA): 阿姆斯特丹-鹿特丹-安特威普——欧洲最重要的石油交易和储存枢纽] jet fuel inventories have dropped 50% since late February. The U.K. appears most `at risk` [at risk: 面临风险——固定搭配] of jet fuel `rationing` [rationing: 配给制/定量供应——战时或短缺时期对物资的分配管制] due to its large net imports.

Lufthansa — Europe's biggest airline group — has already canceled 20,000 flights through October. `Airfare` [airfare: 机票价格] prices are up more than 20% compared to last year.

### The math doesn't add up

Global oil supply will drop to just 98 days of demand by end of May 2026. But that figure is `misleading` [misleading: 具有误导性的].

Grigsby notes that the 98-day figure `overstates` [overstates: 夸大/高估——over+state，说过头了] usable oil because pipelines, refineries, and storage tanks must maintain minimum operational levels. `Floating-roof storage tanks` [floating-roof storage tanks: 浮顶储油罐——一种大型储油设施，罐顶随液面浮动以减少蒸发] need at least 20% capacity to function. Refineries typically require 15 days of `crude` [crude: 原油——crude oil的简称] storage for `smooth operations` [smooth operations: 平稳运转].

The realistic available supply: only 30–40 days of demand.

Jet fuel typically represents only 10% of `refinery output` [refinery output: 炼厂产出], now being `ramped up` [ramped up: 加大/提升——ramp up为常见商业用语，指快速扩大规模] to about 13% — but at the expense of other products.

### Regional devastation

The supply shock has hit Asia hardest:
- South Korea has reduced local jet fuel inventory by **66%**
- Japan reduced by **46%**
- Taiwan and U.K. down by **41%**
- U.S. maintained 100% of local supply
- Europe overall reduced by only 21%

The `discrepancy` [discrepancy: 差异/不一致——dis+crepancy，指数据之间的不匹配] between regions reflects the U.S.'s energy `self-sufficiency` [self-sufficiency: 自给自足] versus Asia and Europe's `dependence on Middle Eastern imports` [dependence on Middle Eastern imports: 对中东进口的依赖].

Industries across Asia are facing a `plastic shortage` [plastic shortage: 塑料短缺] as oil — the `feedstock` [feedstock: 原料——化工行业用语，指用于生产其他产品的基础原料] for `petrochemicals` [petrochemicals: 石化产品] — remains `in short supply` [in short supply: 供应不足/紧缺]. Fortune separately reports a "`Middle East plus one`" [Middle East plus one: "中东+1"——类比"中国+1"战略，指企业为降低对单一地区依赖而分散供应来源] `rethink` [rethink: 重新思考/战略调整] of supply chains is underway.

### Market paradox

Markets celebrated: the S&P 500 hit records, European indices surged (Stoxx 600 +1.55%, FTSE 100 +2.22%), and South Korea's KOSPI soared 6.45%. Brent crude fell from $114 to about $108.

But the `paradox` [paradox: 悖论/矛盾] is stark: while traders price in peace, the `underlying` [underlying: 底层的/根本的] supply crisis remains `acute` [acute: 严重的/尖锐的——医学和经济学常用，指问题紧迫]. The blockade persists despite the `ceasefire` [ceasefire: 停火] announcement. The `fragility` [fragility: 脆弱性] of global energy `infrastructure` [infrastructure: 基础设施] means even `modest` [modest: 适度的/小幅的] supply `disruptions` [disruptions: 中断/干扰] can trigger `cascading shortages` [cascading shortages: 连锁式短缺——像瀑布一样层层传导的供应危机] across multiple industries.

### 重要短语/习语

| 短语 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| twists and turns | 曲折反复 | 用于描述政策/谈判/局势的反复变化 |
| at risk of | 面临...的风险 | "most at risk of rationing" — 正式报告常用 |
| ramp up | 快速扩大/提升 | "being ramped up to about 13%" — 商业/工业领域高频 |
| in short supply | 供应紧缺 | "oil remains in short supply" — 经济新闻标配表达 |
| the math doesn't add up | 算术对不上/逻辑说不通 | 小标题用法，生动形象 |
| Middle East plus one | "中东+1"战略 | 仿造"China plus one"，指供应链多元化 |
| price in | 将...计入价格 | "while traders price in peace" — 金融市场核心概念 |
| stuck in the Gulf | 被困在海湾 | stuck 表示无法脱身的滞留状态 |

### 修辞与写作技巧

1. **对比并置法（Juxtaposition）**：标题"The war is 'over' but the jet fuel crisis is about to begin"——用引号框住'over'暗示质疑，后半句用but立刻翻转，一句话完成"乐观→警示"的叙事张力转换。

2. **数据锤击法（Data hammering）**：连续列出66%、46%、41%、100%、21%的对比数据，不加评论让数字自己说话，这是金融新闻写作的核心技巧——用密集数据建立可信度。

3. **Paradox结构**：文末"while traders price in peace, the underlying supply crisis remains acute"——一句话揭示市场短期乐观与基本面长期隐忧的矛盾，是专业财经评论的经典收束方式。

### 语法亮点

1. **"only to + V"结构**（表示出乎意料的结果）：
   - "The White House has declared the war 'over' multiple times before — only to resume fighting."
   - 含义：之前多次宣布结束，结果都是重新开战。only to表示"（事与愿违地）结果却..."

2. **分词短语作后置修饰语**：
