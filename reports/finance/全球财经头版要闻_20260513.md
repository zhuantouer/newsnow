# 📖 英文原文精读：CNBC — "Wholesale inflation jumps 6% in April on annual basis, biggest increase since 2022"

> **选文理由**：本文是当日最重要的宏观经济数据报道——4月PPI数据直接影响美联储政策走向、债券市场定价和全球风险资产估值，且涉及大量金融/经济领域核心术语，适合精读积累。

## 原文精读（含标注）

**Wholesale inflation** `[wholesale inflation: 批发/生产环节通胀——与CPI（消费者通胀）相对，反映上游价格压力]` **jumps 6% in April on annual basis, biggest increase since 2022**

*Published Wed, May 13, 2026 8:31 AM EDT | Updated 9:21 AM EDT*
*By Jeff Cox*

Wholesale prices rose considerably more than expected in April, another worrisome sign for an economy beset `[beset: 困扰、围攻——"beset by" 是新闻高频搭配，指被某些困难包围]` by stubborn `[stubborn: 此处意为"顽固的"——修饰inflation表示难以消退的通胀]` inflation pressures.

The **producer price index** `[producer price index (PPI): 生产者物价指数——衡量企业出厂价格变动，是CPI的先行指标]`, a measure of what producers get for their goods and services, increased 1.4% on the month, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported Wednesday. That was considerably above the **Dow Jones consensus** `[Dow Jones consensus: 道琼斯共识预期——华尔街分析师平均预测值]` forecast for a 0.5% gain and higher than the upwardly revised `[upwardly revised: 向上修正——前期数据从初值0.5%被修正为0.7%]` March increase of 0.7%.

On a year-over-year basis, the PPI **surged** `[surged: 飙升——比rose/increased/gained程度更强，新闻中用于描述超预期大幅上涨]` 6%, its biggest increase since December 2022.

Excluding food and energy, **core PPI** `[core PPI: 核心PPI——剔除食品和能源后的生产者价格指数，更能反映"底层通胀趋势"]` accelerated 1.0%, also well above the 0.4% estimate. Excluding food, energy and trade services, the index rose 0.6%.

Energy was at the root of the unexpectedly high gain in producer prices, as it was for a surge in consumer prices that the BLS reported a day earlier.

**Final demand energy** `[final demand energy: 最终需求能源——PPI分类术语，指直接面向最终买家的能源产品价格]` jumped 7.8%, accounting for approximately three-quarters of the increase in goods prices. Within the energy category, gasoline prices soared 15.6%, making up more than 40% of the energy increase. **Pump prices** `[pump prices: 加油站零售油价——"pump"指加油枪/加油站]` have soared well past $4 per gallon, a condition attributed to pressures from the Iran war.

On the services side, the index accelerated 1.2%, its largest monthly gain since March 2022. **Trade services** `[trade services: 贸易服务——PPI中的"贸易服务"指批发/零售商的利润率(margins)变化，不是国际贸易]` rose 2.7% and were responsible for about two-thirds of the move in services. The trade services number indicated that **tariff costs are beginning to have a larger impact on prices** `[关税成本开始对价格产生更大影响——暗示企业正将进口关税转嫁给下游]`.

Machinery and equipment wholesaling margins jumped 3.5%.

The report comes a day after the BLS reported that the **consumer price index** `[consumer price index (CPI): 消费者物价指数——衡量终端消费品和服务价格变动]` rose 3.8% from a year ago, primarily driven by surging energy prices, though it also reflected surprisingly high shelter cost increases. **Core CPI** `[core CPI: 核心CPI——剔除食品和能源的消费者价格指数]` ran at 2.8%, still well above the Federal Reserve's 2% target but more subdued than the headline number.

Together, the readings affirm the likelihood that the Fed will **stick to** `[stick to: 坚持、维持——常用于货币政策报道，表示央行不改变立场]` its restrictive monetary policy `[restrictive monetary policy: 紧缩性货币政策——高利率环境以抑制通胀]` for the foreseeable future `[for the foreseeable future: 在可预见的未来——含义为"相当长一段时间内"]`. The central bank has **anchored** `[anchored: 锚定——形象描述利率被固定在某一水平不动]` its benchmark interest rate `[benchmark interest rate: 基准利率——即联邦基金利率，影响全部市场利率]` in a target range between 3.5%-3.75%.

With inflation sticky and the labor market still resilient, there's little chance of interest rate cuts through the rest of 2026, based on **futures market pricing** `[futures market pricing: 期货市场定价——指CME FedWatch等利率期货工具反映的市场隐含概率]`. In fact, the probability of a rate **hike** `[hike: （利率）上调——与"cut"（降息）相对]` climbed to approximately 39% following the PPI report.

The **10-year Treasury yield** `[10-year Treasury yield: 10年期美国国债收益率——全球资产定价之锚]` rose to 4.47%, up from 4.46% the day before and well above the pre-war level of 3.97%.

**David Russell**, Global Head of Market Strategy at TradeStation, stated: "Inflation is sticky and accelerating. The **core reading** `[core reading: 核心数据读数——指剔除波动项后的"干净"通胀数字]` confirms a deeper structural trend, especially in services. The Hormuz crisis is **aggravating** `[aggravating: 加剧——程度比worsening更强，带有"雪上加霜"的含义]` the problem, but this goes way beyond oil."

## 📝 学习笔记

### 核心金融词汇

| 单词/短语 | 含义 | 例句语境 |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Producer Price Index (PPI) | 生产者物价指数 | "The PPI increased 1.4% on the month" |
| wholesale inflation | 批发环节通胀 | "Wholesale prices rose considerably more than expected" |
| core PPI/CPI | 核心PPI/CPI（剔除食品能源） | "core PPI accelerated 1.0%" |
| benchmark interest rate | 基准利率 | "anchored its benchmark interest rate in a target range" |
| restrictive monetary policy | 紧缩性货币政策 | "stick to its restrictive monetary policy" |
| futures market pricing | 期货市场定价/隐含概率 | "based on futures market pricing" |
| 10-year Treasury yield | 10年期美债收益率 | "rose to 4.47%" |
| trade services | PPI中的贸易服务（批发零售利润率） | "Trade services rose 2.7%" |
| final demand | 最终需求 | "Final demand energy jumped 7.8%" |
| Dow Jones consensus | 道琼斯共识预期 | "above the Dow Jones consensus forecast" |

### 重要短语/习语

| 短语 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| beset by | 被...困扰/围攻 | "an economy beset by stubborn inflation" |
| stick to | 坚持、维持不变 | "the Fed will stick to its restrictive policy" |
| on a year-over-year basis | 同比（与去年同期相比） | "surged 6% on a year-over-year basis" |
| for the foreseeable future | 在可预见的未来 | "stick to its restrictive monetary policy for the foreseeable future" |
| at the root of | 是...的根本原因 | "Energy was at the root of the unexpectedly high gain" |
| well above | 远高于 | "well above the Federal Reserve's 2% target" |
| goes way beyond | 远远超出...的范畴 | "this goes way beyond oil" |

### 修辞与写作技巧

1. **数据对比强化震撼力**：文章反复使用"expected X, actual Y"的结构——"considerably above the forecast for 0.5%"（预期0.5%/实际1.4%）——三倍差距本身就是新闻
2. **因果链层层递进**：Iran war → oil prices → gasoline → final demand energy (+7.8%) → 3/4 of goods increase → trade services (+2.7%) → tariff pass-through。每一环都有数据支撑
3. **引语点睛**：全文仅一处直接引语放在结尾，作为对全文数据的"升华性概括"——"this goes way beyond oil"一句将读者思维从"能源问题"提升到"结构性通胀"
4. **动词梯度**：rose → accelerated → surged → soared → jumped——描述涨幅时动词强度递增，映射数据偏离预期的程度

### 语法亮点

1. **分词短语作后置状语**：
   - "another worrisome sign for an economy **beset by** stubborn inflation pressures" — 过去分词短语修饰economy

2. **非限定性定语从句省略**：
   - "as it was for a surge in consumer prices that the BLS reported a day earlier" — as引导的比较从句+that定语从句嵌套

3. **with + 名词 + 形容词（独立主格结构）**：
   - "**With** inflation sticky **and** the labor market still resilient, there's little chance of..." — 表伴随条件/原因

4. **it was...that 强调句变体**：
   - "Energy was at the root of the unexpectedly high gain" — 将主语前置强调
