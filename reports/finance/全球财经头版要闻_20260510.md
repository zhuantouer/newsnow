- **海峡实质封锁持续**：据Oman Observer报道，美伊近日海上交火后霍尔木兹海峡航行仍被严重限制，双方相互实施的"竞争性封锁"使通行量维持极低水平

### 2. 美联储Goolsbee：加息重回桌面——Bloomberg/Briefs

芝加哥联储主席Austan Goolsbee在5月10日（文章发布日）告诉Bloomberg电视台：**所有利率选项——包括加息——仍然可能**。这标志着美联储鸽派代表人物的立场根本性转变。

**关键要点：**
- 4月底FOMC会议维持利率不变（3.50%-3.75%），但**三位官员投反对票**，反对声明中暗示下一步仅为降息的措辞
- Goolsbee称通胀压力**已从能源冲击蔓延到非能源领域**——商品和服务价格均在上升
- 此前5月7日他还警告：若AI投资热潮推动支出但生产率未跟上，联储可能需要加息

### 3. 经济影响：能源危机深度传导

- **Fed金融稳定报告**（5月9日发布）：将地缘政治冲突+石油供应冲击列为**头号系统性风险**

## 📖 英文原文精读：Fed's Goolsbee Puts Rate Hikes Back On The Table

**来源：Briefs Finance | 作者：Nate Gregory | 发布日期：May 10, 2026**

> 选择理由：此文直击本日最重要的宏观政策转向——美联储从"何时降息"转为"可能加息"，影响全球资产定价逻辑。篇幅精炼、金融术语密集，适合精读。

---

### 原文精读（含标注）

**Fed's Goolsbee Just Said Rate Hikes Are Back On The Table**

Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee told Bloomberg Television that all interest rate options — including hikes `[hikes: 加息——此处指利率上调，与 cuts（降息）对应]` — are still possible.

The Fed kept rates unchanged `[kept rates unchanged: 维持利率不变——标准表述 "hold rates steady" 的变体]` at its end-of-April meeting, with three officials dissenting `[dissenting: 投反对票/持异议——FOMC会议中，dissent 指投票与多数派不同]` in favor of language allowing for either a cut or a hike.

Goolsbee said inflation worries from the energy-price shock `[energy-price shock: 能源价格冲击——指伊朗战争导致的油价暴涨对整体物价的传导效应]` tied to the US war in Iran are reaching beyond just energy `[reaching beyond just energy: 蔓延到能源以外——即通胀从能源扩散到核心商品和服务]`.

**Why This Matters:**

Goolsbee has historically been one of the Fed's more dovish `[dovish: 鸽派的——倾向宽松货币政策/降息。反义词：hawkish（鹰派/倾向紧缩）]` voices — someone inclined `[inclined: 倾向于——常用搭配 be inclined to do sth]` to err on the side of `[err on the side of: 宁可偏向……一侧——习语，表示在不确定时选择更谨慎/更宽松的方向]` cutting rates rather than holding them high. So when *he* says rate hikes can't be ruled out `[can't be ruled out: 不能排除——高频金融/政策表达，"rule out" 意为排除可能性]`, it signals `[signals: 发出信号/预示——金融语境中指政策制定者通过言论暗示未来方向]` a notable shift in the committee's thinking.

**What Changed — Two Key Factors:**

**1. The Committee Split `[committee split: 委员会分裂——指FOMC内部意见不统一]`**

At the most recent meeting, three members pushed back against `[pushed back against: 反对/抵制——比 "disagree" 更强烈，暗示主动施压]` the statement's phrasing `[phrasing: 措辞/用语——指声明的具体文字表述]`, which implied the next move would be a cut. They wanted the door left open for a hike too. Three dissenters is rare `[rare: 罕见的——通常FOMC决策为一致或仅1-2人反对，3人反对极为少见]` — it shows genuine internal disagreement, not just one outlier `[outlier: 离群值/异类——金融/统计术语，此处比喻少数派不是孤例]`.

**2. Inflation Spreading Beyond Energy**

The initial concern was that oil prices — driven up by the Iran war — would be a "transitory" shock `["transitory" shock: "暂时性"冲击——呼应2021年通胀争论中的经典用语，暗示Fed最初低估了持续性]`. But Goolsbee acknowledged that price pressures `[price pressures: 物价压力——通胀的同义表达]` are now showing up in non-energy goods and services. That's the definition of inflation becoming entrenched `[entrenched: 根深蒂固的——原义为"构筑战壕"，比喻通胀已难以撼动]`.

**What It Means for Markets:**

- **Stocks**: Much of the recent rally has been underpinned by `[underpinned by: 以……为支撑——under + pin，比喻为基础/支柱]` expectations of eventual rate cuts making money cheaper. If hikes are genuinely on the table, that pillar `[pillar: 支柱——比喻支撑市场的核心逻辑]` weakens.
- **Bonds**: Treasury yields `[Treasury yields: 国债收益率——美国国债的到期收益率]` could rise sharply if markets start pricing in `[pricing in: 将……计入价格——金融术语，指市场预期已反映在资产价格中]` a tighter policy path `[tighter policy path: 更紧缩的政策路径——指加息方向]`.
- **The Dollar**: A hawkish `[hawkish: 鹰派的——倾向紧缩货币政策/加息]` Fed typically strengthens the dollar, which could put pressure on commodities `[commodities: 大宗商品——石油、黄金、铜等实物商品]` and emerging markets.

**What To Watch:**

The next FOMC meeting will be critical `[critical: 关键的/至关重要的]`. If more members adopt the "either direction" framing `[framing: 框架/话术——指表述政策方向的方式]`, expect bond markets to reprice quickly `[reprice quickly: 快速重新定价——债市对利率预期变化反应极快]`.

### 📝 学习笔记

#### 核心金融词汇

| 单词/短语 | 含义 | 例句语境 |
|-----------|------|----------|
| rate hike | 加息 | "Rate hikes are back on the table" |
| dissent / dissenting | 反对票/持异议 | "three officials dissenting in favor of..." |
| dovish / hawkish | 鸽派/鹰派 | "one of the Fed's more dovish voices" |
| pricing in | 计入价格/反映预期 | "markets start pricing in a tighter policy path" |
| entrenched | 根深蒂固的 | "inflation becoming entrenched" |
| transitory | 暂时性的 | "a transitory shock" — 2021年Fed经典误判用语 |
| Treasury yields | 国债收益率 | "Treasury yields could rise sharply" |
| reprice | 重新定价 | "bond markets to reprice quickly" |
| outlier | 离群值/异类 | "not just one outlier" |
| underpinned by | 以……为支撑 | "rally has been underpinned by expectations of rate cuts" |

#### 重要短语/习语

| 短语 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| back on the table | 重新被纳入考虑 | 标题直接使用，表示此前被排除的选项回归 |
| err on the side of | 宁可偏向某一侧 | "err on the side of cutting rates" — 在不确定时倾向降息 |
| can't be ruled out | 不能排除 | 政策/金融高频表达 |
| pushed back against | 反对/抵制 | 比 disagree 更强烈的对抗性表达 |
| leave the door open | 留有余地 | "They wanted the door left open for a hike" |
| on the table | 在讨论范围内 | 与 "off the table"（不在考虑范围内）对应 |

#### 修辞与写作技巧

1. **对比强化（Contrast for emphasis）**：文章反复使用 dovish ↔ hawkish, cut ↔ hike, transitory ↔ entrenched 的对比，让政策转向的戏剧性跃然纸上
2. **信号词层次递进**："signals → shows → acknowledged" 动词力度递增，暗示确定性在增强
3. **先设定预期再打破**（Setup & Payback）："Goolsbee has historically been dovish... So when *he* says hikes can't be ruled out..." — 先建立读者对人物立场的认知，再用转折 "So when *he*" 制造冲击
4. **类比思维**："That pillar weakens" — 将市场逻辑比作建筑支柱，形象传达结构性风险

#### 语法亮点

1. **with + 名词 + 现在分词（独立主格结构）**：
   - "The Fed kept rates unchanged, **with three officials dissenting** in favor of..."
   - 此结构用于补充说明伴随情况，比 "and three officials dissented" 更紧凑

2. **if引导的条件句与情态动词组合**：
   - "**If** more members adopt..., expect bond markets **to** reprice quickly"
   - 省略了主语 "you"（祈使句+条件句混合），新闻/分析文常见

3. **动词-ing 作后置定语**：
   - "price pressures **showing up** in non-energy goods"
   - 动态描写，比 "that show up" 更简洁流畅
